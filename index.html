See your film negatives as positives, live.

lup turns your iPhone into a loupe. Hold it over a negative on a lightbox, and the picture appears — the way you used to check a contact sheet, only closer.

Coming soon to the App Store

How to use

  1. Place the negative on a lightbox, or on a white screen on an iPad or computer.
  2. Hold your iPhone over the negative. The picture appears as a positive.
  3. Press the shutter button to save it. Tap the thumbnail to zoom in and share it.

What it does

  • Works with color and black-and-white negatives.
  • Removes the orange cast of color film with one tap.
  • Zoom, brightness, and contrast on a dial you can turn with one thumb, so your other hand can hold the film flat.
  • No ads, no subscription, and no data collected.

Colors are approximate and vary with your light source and film. lup is for checking focus, framing, and exposure. For accurate color, scan your negatives.

/lup/ is how loupe sounds in French: the small magnifier photographers have always used to look at film.
